FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about ticket bookings and policies.

How do I download or print my e-ticket? ▼
Immediately upon completing your payment, your official e-ticket boarding pass is displayed. You can also visit My Bookings anytime to view, download as PDF, or print your ticket.
How does cancellation and seat restoration work? ▼
Navigate to My Bookings, locate your confirmed reservation, and click "Cancel Ticket". The system immediately cancels the booking, updates your status, and automatically frees up the seats back into the available pool for other travelers.
What documents are required while boarding? ▼
Any government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar Card, Driving License, Passport, or Voter ID) along with your digital E-ticket or printed boarding pass is required.
Can I book multiple seats in one go? ▼
Yes! You can select up to 6 seats in a single reservation. The system will dynamically generate passenger input fields for each chosen seat and calculate the total fare accordingly.
