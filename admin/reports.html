🎓 College Mini-Project Demonstration Notes

• All metrics are dynamically computed directly from the browser's localStorage database.
• When you book a seat as a customer, the gross revenue and active booking counts automatically increment.
• When a booking is cancelled, the ticket status changes to "Cancelled", seats are freed, and metrics adjust.
• The "Export All Bookings (CSV)" button generates a standard RFC-4180 CSV document that opens directly in Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets.
